Concept Scope Guard, a proposed extension of Dela in Deltek Vantagepoint, by Anirudh Chennamaneni Code and write-upThought process

Dela tells you a project is losing money. This tells you why, and what to bill.

An enhancement, not a new product. Dela's policy review already checks timesheets against company policy and flags exceptions to a person. Scope Guard points the same pattern at the signed contract: it reads every timesheet note, flags work outside the scope with the clause and the evidence, totals the unbilled value by cause and drafts the change request. The project manager reviews every flag. Nothing reaches the client without them.

How it works

    

      
Read the notes
Every timesheet note from Vantagepoint, with phase, person, hours and rate. Scope clauses come from Dela's existing contract summaries.
Data

      
Match to the contract
An LLM labels each note in scope, out of scope or unclear, cites one clause and quotes the evidence.
AI

      
Total the value
Hours times billing rate, grouped by cause. Plain arithmetic, so the numbers can be audited.
Rules

      
PM decides
Flags appear in the Dela Insights panel the PM already uses. They agree or override each call, then send the request. Overrides feed the eval set.
Human

    

  


  

    
Why it isn't built yet
Three good reasons not to have built this, and how the design answers each

    

      
Trade-off
The data is weak
Timesheet notes are often optional and vague, and contract scope usually lives in a signed PDF.
Answer: fix the data first. When someone logs time through Dela, it asks one question: "Who asked for this?" Scope clauses come from Dela's contract summaries. Phase 0 measures note quality before anything ships.

      
Trade-off
Being wrong in front of a client is costly
A false flag that reaches a client hurts the firm and Deltek's credibility.
Answer: internal only. Flags go to the PM, like policy review goes to approvers. Every call shows its evidence, and nothing leaves the firm without a person sending it.

      
Focus
Bigger bets came first
Proposals and month-end close apply to every customer in every segment. Scope creep mostly hurts fixed-fee A&E work.
Answer: start narrow. Fixed-fee projects on Vantagepoint cloud only, where the dollars are clearest and the feature gives firms a reason to be on cloud.

    

  


  

    
How we'd know it's working
North star: adoption that comes with value

      
Deltek's own research: 70% of A&E firms use AI, but only 38% see measurable impact. So the north star counts PMs who act on what Dela tells them, not people who glance at it.
